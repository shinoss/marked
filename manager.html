        Settings

        Appearance

        Marked follows your computer’s light or dark mode, unless you choose one here.

        Browser bookmarks

        Copy the bookmarks saved in your browser into Marked, keeping their folders. Ones already in Marked are skipped, and nothing changes in the browser.

        Page text

        Marked keeps the readable text of the pages you save, so search finds any words in them, and you still have them if a page changes or disappears. The text stays on this device.

        Saved articles you open again get their text then, if they don’t have it yet. Other saved pages, like an inbox or an account page, keep their text only when you save them yourself.

        Browsing

        What the Marked button shows while you browse. It’s all worked out on this device.

        The button shows a number when your saved pages share telling words with the page’s title and headings. Choose the button to see them.

        Semantic search

        Search your bookmarks by meaning with TypeSafe’s Jev model, using your own API key. Choose Semantic beside the search box to run a search.

        What each search sends

        Your query and one short line per bookmark: its title and abstract, plus what you choose here. Addresses, folders, and tags are never sent.

        Preview

        Works without a key: each search logs the request Jev would receive to the browser console, and nothing is sent.

        Usage

        Jev usage

        Estimated from the tokens TypeSafe reports, at $0.042 per million input tokens (output is free). Your TypeSafe console has the exact bill.
